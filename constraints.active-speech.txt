# Shared pins for the recorder, Faster-Whisper and WhisperX images and their
# integration lanes. Other model families have separate compatibility requirements.

# Torch family. The images preinstall the CUDA 12.8 build of Torch 2.8:
# CTranslate2 (Faster-Whisper) loads CUDA 12 cuBLAS from Torch's NVIDIA wheels,
# and TorchCodec 0.7 and WhisperX 3.8 support only Torch 2.8. Without these pins
# pip may replace the preinstalled build with a newer PyPI release for another
# CUDA major version when a transitive dependency asks for a newer Torch.
# "==2.8.0" also matches the local +cu128 and +cpu builds.
